Granite types for headstones and memorial monuments

Granite is durable, low-maintenance and available in many colours and natural patterns. Because every slab is unique, photographs provide an indication rather than an exact colour guarantee.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Is granite suitable for outdoor use?

Yes. Granite is widely used for memorials because it is strong and resistant to weathering.

Will the colour match the photograph exactly?

Natural variation and screen settings can affect colour. The final stone may show differences in pattern and tone.

Can different materials be combined?

Yes. Granite can be combined with glass, stainless steel, bronze and other decorative elements.
