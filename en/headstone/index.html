Which material is most commonly used?

Granite is widely chosen for its durability and range of colours, but marble and other materials are also available.

Can I send an example through WhatsApp?

Yes. Send a photograph, sketch or description and we will discuss the available options.

Yes. Dimensions and installation requirements can differ, so the cemetery regulations must be checked.
