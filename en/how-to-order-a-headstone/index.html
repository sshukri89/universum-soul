Your headstone step by step

How to order a custom headstone

Ordering a headstone involves more than choosing a model. We help you make considered decisions about cemetery rules, material, lettering, accessories, price and installation.

Request personal adviceView our work

1. Share your wishes

Tell us about the preferred style, grave type, cemetery, text and available budget.

2. Design and materials

We discuss the layout and compare suitable stone types, colours and finishes.

3. Approval and production

The design, spelling and price are confirmed before production begins.

4. Delivery and installation

The completed monument is delivered and installed according to the cemetery requirements.

Personal guidance throughout the Netherlands

Universum Soul works from Amsterdam and assists families throughout the Netherlands. We discuss the design, material, lettering and practical cemetery requirements before production. This keeps the process clear and prevents assumptions about what is included.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What information should I provide first?

The cemetery, grave type, approximate dimensions, preferred material, text and any examples are useful.

When is the final text checked?

All names, dates and inscriptions must be approved before production.

How long does the process take?

Timing depends on design approval, material availability, production and cemetery permissions. You receive an estimate in advance.
